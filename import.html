Fill this in, click Make the code, then copy the result into data.js. The site does the rest.

How to get it on your site
  1. Fill in the form and press Make the code
  2. Press Copy, then open data.js in Notepad or TextEdit
  3. Find the line "movies": [ and paste your code right after the [
  4. Add a comma , at the very end of your pasted block
  5. Save, upload the folder again — your site updates
Put your own artwork in the images/posters folder, or paste a web address.
